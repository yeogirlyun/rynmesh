Product inventory · v0.7.0

One local node. A reader, an AI, and a circle of friends.

These statuses describe the public v0.7.0 source. Alpha means the behavior works and is tested, but contracts and storage may still change before 1.0.

Available in alpha

For You recommendations without setup

Built-in public sources provide video, article, research, podcast, audiobook and visual picks from the first launch, ranked locally without a model.

Available in alpha

Direction, preferences and feedback

Written direction plus topic and platform controls guide discovery. More, Less, Hide, opens and bookmarks update a profile that lives on your disk.

Available in alpha

First reading and bookmarks

Read and bookmark real content right after install. Reading history, progress and read-later links are stored locally and can be exported or erased.

Available in alpha

Offline reading

Download articles and their images and read them without a network connection. Clearing a download forgets its title and link too.

Available in alpha

Multi-device sync

Pair a second computer with a code you read off the other screen. Reading progress, bookmarks and conversations sync over an encrypted link; conflicts are shown, never guessed.

Available in alpha

Ask Ryn

Ask about the article you are reading, keep the history, switch models from one entry point. Cancel, retry and timeouts are honest about what the node confirmed.

Available in alpha

Local AI and friend permissions

Enable a bundled local model with one click and decide, friend by friend, who may use it. A cloud provider is used only with explicit permission and your own credentials.

Available in alpha

Friend pairing and sharing

Invite with a link or QR code after reviewing the endpoint, share an article, chat with attachments, and revoke at any time. Works across NATs through a sealed relay mailbox.

Available in alpha

Follow friends' updates

A separate feed of what friends choose to share, with unread markers, subscription control and cleanup.

Available in alpha

Search and retrieve

One search recovers your content, friends' shares and chat history, and tells you when an item could not be indexed.

Hardening

Content viewer

Articles, images, feed-provided audio and YouTube items open with recommendation evidence. Accessibility, PDF support and media privacy are active work.

Hardening

Daily Digest

Signed publishing

Publish files with signed manifests and provenance evidence; peers verify hashes before anything propagates.

Important boundaries

What the release does not promise.

RynMesh is alpha software. It is not a network of strangers, its credits are not money, and the macOS packages are not Apple-notarized.

01

No model required for recommendations

Generated briefings and answers do require a local or explicitly configured model.

02

No peer required for personal use

Friends and device sync are optional; the reader works alone.

03

No silent cloud profile

Feedback, history and conversations stay in the node's local state.

04

No strangers

Only people you invite can reach your node. Open discovery waits for the safety work in milestone 4.
