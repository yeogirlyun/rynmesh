# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the Rynmesh community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include demonstrating empathy, respecting differing viewpoints, giving and accepting constructive feedback, taking responsibility for mistakes, and focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes sexualized language or attention, trolling or insulting comments, harassment, publishing another person's private information without permission, and other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing acceptable behavior. They may remove, edit, or reject contributions, comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within project spaces and whenever an individual officially represents the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior privately through [GitHub's private reporting form](https://github.com/yeogirlyun/rynmesh/security/advisories/new) with “Code of Conduct” in the title. Reports will be reviewed promptly and kept confidential to the extent practical.

Maintainers will follow the Contributor Covenant enforcement guidelines, using correction, warning, temporary ban, or permanent ban according to the impact and pattern of behavior.
